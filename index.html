About me

I architect and deploy production AI systems with a focus on LLM agents, RAG pipelines, and evaluation infrastructure. Most AI engineers stop at the model. I build the measurement, CI/CD, and observability that make AI trustworthy at scale.

It's fairly easy to build a demo that works 80% of the time. The hard part is getting to 95% and knowing when you're there, which is why so much of my work is evaluation.

At VideoAmp I drove LLMOps and agent infrastructure end to end: an LLM-as-judge evaluation framework, MCP servers with Progressive Discovery, and Go/Python agents on Bedrock. Before that I led healthcare, cybersecurity, and finance AI projects at CloudWerx, including an agentic system that cut patient-note writing from 7 minutes to 2. I hold an MS in Data Science from NYU and engineering degrees from Universidad de Chile.
